The firm informs you of its fees before any engagement, whether for:

- a one-off consultation,
- a retainer agreement,
- amicable negotiation proceedings,
- legal proceedings.

The firm's services are billed based on time spent. For legal proceedings, a flat fee is applied, supplemented, depending on the nature of the case, by a success fee.

In addition, the details of these fees will always be set out in writing, in the form of a fee agreement, for any matter entrusted or retainer agreed with the firm.
