Having built her experience within large groups and business law firms, Nathalie Brackmann understands the demands of corporate advisory work and the importance of building a **relationship of trust** with her various contacts.

The firm therefore offers **tailored advice**, adapted to the profile of its contacts (CEO, CFO, HR Director, Director of Labour Relations, etc.), their sector of activity (new technologies, finance, hospitality, catering, laundry services, personal services, etc.) and the size of their company (small businesses, SMEs, or subsidiaries of larger groups).

The firm is regularly called upon to act in the following situations:

To protect your interests, you may wish to:

- carry out a **social audit** ahead of an upcoming acquisition, or to bring your company into compliance with existing labour legislation,
- be assisted in **drafting legal documents** such as employment contracts, warnings, dismissal letters, settlement agreements, information notices for your employee representative bodies, internal regulations, or collective agreements,
- receive advice on the **applicable procedure** for a current or upcoming situation,
- negotiate a **settlement agreement**, either before or during labour court proceedings,
- be assisted in your **dealings with the labour inspectorate, occupational physician or URSSAF**.

To defend your interests, you may wish to:

- be assisted or represented in proceedings before the Labour Court, the various amicable appeal boards, or the Judicial Court,
- consider the merits of bringing legal proceedings or appealing a decision unfavourable to you,
- be assisted or represented in appeal proceedings.
