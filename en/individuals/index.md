The firm is regularly called upon to act in the following situations:

To protect your interests, you may wish to obtain advice on:

- an **employment contract** that has been offered to you,
- the possible **reclassification** of your fixed-term contract as a permanent contract,
- the terms of **performance** of your employment contract,
- the terms of its possible **termination**,
- **challenging** the termination of your employment contract.

To defend your interests, you may wish to:

- consider the merits of taking legal action against your employer,
- be assisted or represented in a dispute with your employer,
- consider the merits of appealing a decision unfavourable to you,
- be assisted or represented in appeal proceedings.
