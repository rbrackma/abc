    <div class="section__head">
      <p class="section__kicker">Mediation</p>
      <h2>Finding a solution together</h2>
      <p class="page-intro">Mediation is an amicable way of resolving disputes. The mediator, an independent and impartial third party, helps the parties restore dialogue and build a solution suited to their needs themselves. The mediator does not decide the dispute and does not give legal advice, and each party may be assisted by their lawyer.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card-grid card-grid--2">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Contractual mediation</h3>
        <p>It is initiated by the parties, before or outside any court proceedings. They choose together to call on a mediator to resolve a disagreement quickly and confidentially. The agreement reached can be submitted to a judge to make it enforceable.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Court-ordered mediation</h3>
        <p>It is ordered by the judge, with the parties' consent, during proceedings. It lasts three months, renewable once. If an agreement is reached, the judge can approve it; otherwise, the proceedings resume.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
