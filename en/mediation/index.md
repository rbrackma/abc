    <div class="section__head">
      <h2>What is mediation?</h2>
      <p class="page-intro">Mediation is an amicable way of resolving disputes. The mediator, an independent and impartial third party, <strong>helps the parties restore dialogue and build a solution</strong> suited to their needs themselves. The discussions are confidential. The parties may be assisted by their lawyers and record the commitments made during mediation in a settlement agreement. They may also choose to have this agreement approved by a judge to make it enforceable.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card-grid card-grid--2">
      <div class="card">
        <h3 class="card__title--accent">Contractual mediation</h3>
        <p>It is <strong>initiated by the parties, outside any court proceedings or alongside ongoing proceedings</strong>. The parties thus choose to call on a mediator with a view to resolving a disagreement quickly and confidentially.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3 class="card__title--accent">Court-ordered mediation</h3>
        <p>It is <strong>ordered by the judge, with the parties' consent, during proceedings</strong>. It must be completed within five months and may be extended for a further three months. If an agreement is reached, the parties may choose to have it approved by the judge to make it enforceable. If no agreement is reached, the proceedings resume.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
