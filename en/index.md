It appears in particular before the Labour Courts (Conseils de prud'hommes) of <strong>Grasse</strong>, <strong>Cannes</strong> and <strong>Nice</strong>, the Judicial Court of Grasse or Nice, and the Court of Appeal of <strong>Aix-en-Provence</strong>.</p>
        <p>Nathalie Brackmann has extensive experience in <strong>advising companies</strong>, gained through her practice of labour and employment law at the Paris Bar in leading French and Anglo-Saxon business law firms, and then within an international group in the restaurant sector. This experience covers both day-to-day human resources management and more sensitive or large-scale projects.</p>
